Who We Are

About Objective: Cancer

Registered 501(c)3 Nonprofit

Objective: Cancer is a student-led nonprofit focused on raising awareness for cancer in all its forms and promoting proactiveness in early detection and prevention.

We believe that knowledge saves lives. Through our Outreach Initiative, social media campaigns, and community events, we make sure life-saving cancer information reaches people where they are.

Outreach Initiative in Action

Our most recent visits — delivering care packages directly to cancer patients in Boston.

May 2025

Hope Lodge Boston

We delivered care packages to cancer patients staying at the American Cancer Society's Hope Lodge — a free housing facility for patients undergoing treatment away from home.

May 2025

Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center

Our team visited Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center to hand-deliver care packages to oncology staff and patients, each with a personalized card from our members.
